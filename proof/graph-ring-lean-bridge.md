## Stage A: graph ring and centered algebra

`GraphRing.lean` packages arbitrary finite simple undirected graphs using an actual
finite vertex type and a Mathlib `SimpleGraph`. Empty and disconnected graphs are
included. Disjoint union is Mathlib's graph sum; multiplication is its Cartesian
`boxProd`, not its tensor/direct product.

`GraphRing` is the free commutative ring on these graphs, quotiented by the ideal
generated by exactly the empty, K₁, isomorphism, sum and Cartesian-product
relations. `liftInvariant` and `graphRing_hom_ext` prove the universal ring-valued
invariant extension property and uniqueness. No walk or symbol identity is a
presentation relation. `epsilon` descends vertex count through every relation;
in particular it maps K₁ to 1, so the presentation is not the zero ring.

`Augmentation.lean` proves:

- Actual graph classes span the ring **additively** over ℤ. This is stronger than
  ring generation and uses the Cartesian-product relation.
- `center x = x - epsilon(x)`, `centered G = [G] - |V(G)|[K₁]`, and
  `augmentationIdeal = ker epsilon`.
- The centered actual graphs span this ideal additively.
- For **positive** q, `(I^q).restrictScalars ℤ` is the additive span of products of
  exactly q centered actual graph classes. The q=0 boundary is deliberately
  excluded: the empty product alone spans ℤ·1, while I⁰ is the whole ring.
- `vanishes_on_power_iff` transfers vanishing on all such products to every
  virtual graph-ring element in I^q, for any ℤ-linear invariant.

`CenteredSupport.lean` proves the centered inclusion–exclusion expansion **in the
constructed ring**, its evaluation under a ℤ-linear map, and pointwise support
cancellation. T denotes removed coordinates; its weight is
`(-1)^|T| product_{i in T} |V(G_i)|`. The empty-subset term is retained; dropping
it requires a separately proved invariant value at K₁. `support_cancellation`
cancels one supported object, and `full_support_extraction` exchanges the finite
sums to extract exactly full support for any finite object family.

## Formal obligations after Stage A

The generic extraction lemma does **not** itself identify graph-count evaluations
with the relevant object family. That correspondence must still be proved.

1. Actual Cartesian trajectories, unique coordinate recovery, cyclic NB including
   the seam, arbitrary-factor support budget, and maximal-support edge recovery.
2. A bijection between maximal-support factor walks and edge data × the **existing**
   `Word k` type. Its multiplicity must be derived from checked finite cardinal
   equivalences, consuming `word_count`/`rooted_count`, not from an assumed symbol.
3. A ℤ-linear graph-ring descent of the actual cyclic NB count; fiber restrictions
   preserving adjacency and the cyclic seam; identification with the checked
   centered sum. The ring-valued invariant universal property alone does not
   construct a nonmultiplicative walk invariant.
4. Combine 1–3 with `vanishes_on_power_iff` for the global upper filtration bound
   and symbol. Extend factor identities by multilinearity; establish the nonzero
   K₂ evaluation separately before claiming exact order.
5. Primitive trace/Möbius normalization and simple-cycle/aggregate-deficit cases
   remain separate. In particular eta₂=eta₃=0 gives no exact-order assertion.
