The historical `Q7` value is not a harmless rounding error: it violates
the nonnegativity of the full-coordinate-support defect. With
`B_j=c14^{full}(Q_j)`, the exact face decomposition is

`c14(Q_n) = sum_{j=0}^n binom(n,j) 2^(n-j) B_j`.

At full support, `B_7=2^7 7! * 38,232 / 28 = 880,865,280`, and the
lower-support contribution is `2,456,348,160`, summing to the
correct `3,337,213,440`. The historical seventh finite difference is
therefore invalid. The claimed sign reversal and inferred oscillation must
be deleted from any revised paper (v4 is preserved unaltered).

On the full ring of finite simple graphs with ordinary non-induced counts,
the separate proof record gives

`ord_epsilon(c_(2k)) = k;`
`sigma_k(c_(2k)) = gamma_k d^tensor k,`
`gamma_k = 2^k k! a_k/(4k) > 0` for every `k >= 2`,

where `a_k` counts the canonical simple, full-support paired flip words.
Consequently the historical question whether a `gamma_k` vanishes is
resolved *within these conventions*. This theorem remains a **working**
claim in the registry pending independent mathematical/literature acceptance.

## P1 — nonbacktracking proof obligations remain

The historical v4 made meaningful progress: the irregular Bass trace
identity is explicit, and Proposition 4.5 derives
`nb4=w4−2 sum_v deg(v)^2+2|E|` algebraically rather than by curve-fitting.
That identity can be evaluated independently on its own merits.

However, **Lemma 4.7 / Proposition 4.8 have not yet established the claimed
all-k global nonbacktracking principal symbol**:

1. The trace-algebra induction on word degree does not fully control
   filtration-order bounds for every trace word or every cancellation
   among their linear combinations. Product expansions and leading symbols
   alone do not prove that the associated graded algebra is generated by
   `d` in degree one.
2. In particular, Proposition 4.8 infers a nonzero `k`-linear evaluation
   from the cube coefficient but gives no independent proof that
   `nb_(2k) in Diff^k_epsilon` on arbitrary bipartite factors. The claim
   `ord(nb_(2k))=k` needs **both** an upper bound and a nonzero witness.
3. The Gaussian-Chebyshev positivity lemma claims an elementary lower
   bound `J_3 > 1.275` without exhibiting a certified rational/integral
   bound. The numeric inequality is plausible, but it needs a recorded
   calculation or a replacement finite proof.
4. For the trace algebra, `tr(Q)(K1)=-1`; the paper uses an uncentered
   iterated Leibniz defect as its symbol. The repository proof record
   carefully distinguishes centered `D_qf` from uncentered
   `delta^(q-1) f(x_q)`. Normalization must be reconciled before combining
   high-order lemmas. This distinction does not change the ordinary
   cycle `N_H(K1)=0` computation but matters for trace monomials.

**Required validation:** prove the upper bound and pure symbol without
assuming a false associated-graded generation implication. Use an explicit
finite word/edge-support classification or a fully developed coalgebraic
filtration proof; then independently replay the matrix trace calculation
on nonregular bipartite products. Until then, the primitive
`p_(2k)` all-k symbol (dependent on Proposition 4.8) is **conditional**,
although its divisor/Möbius filtration identity is sound under the
appropriate order hypothesis.

## P1 — deficit/junction recursion remains conjectural

`Delta_k = p_(2k) - 2 c_(2k)` is a valid distinction between oriented
primitive nonbacktracking cycles and simple cycle copies. The (k=4)
figure-eight mechanism is concrete. The general recursion by partitions
of `k` into parts `>=2` and fitted `mu_lambda` remains an exploratory
model, **not** a universal decomposition theorem. Primitive
nonbacktracking closed walks can have more complicated repeated-vertex
incidence than bouquets of disjoint simple cycles; quotienting to
partition types may discard necessary junction data.

At `k=7`, the **corrected** deficit symbol on `K2` tuples, if the
stated `beta_7=28,507,207,680` and Möbius coefficient are accepted, is

`D_7(Delta_7) = beta_7/14 - 2*880,865,280 = 274,498,560`,

not the historical value inferred from the negative cycle coefficient.
It remains necessary to prove the *global* `Delta_7` symbol structure.
