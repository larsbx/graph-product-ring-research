# Research roadmap

## Phase 0 — authority and provenance

- import the current manuscript source and compiled PDF;
- import the verification package and methods documentation;
- record source hashes and the manuscript version;
- reconcile all theorem labels with `proof/claims.toml`;
- preserve retractions/corrections from earlier drafts.

Exit: every advertised result has an exact repository locator and status.

## Phase 1 — prior-art correction

- replace unrestricted “fiber-capture” novelty language with Cartesian S-prime
  terminology and citations;
- remove any claim that unrestricted S-prime characterization or recognition
  complexity is open;
- distinguish Cartesian-prime from Cartesian S-prime throughout;
- incorporate the N6 literature audit and bibliography.

Exit: manuscript and docs agree with the October 2026 prior-art gate.

## Phase 2 — exact invariant reproduction

Reproduce independently:

1. (c_4) product formula;
2. (P_3) product formula;
3. full (c_6) product formula;
4. iterated-commutator extraction of their claimed top symbols;
5. bipartite walk-symbol theorem fixtures;
6. exact triangle-free theorem statement and fixtures.

Exit: each finite/checkable claim has a replayable receipt and at least one
independent oracle path where feasible.

## Phase 3 — residual novelty gates

- complete `novelty-lab#32` for exact cycle/walk claims;
- complete `novelty-lab#33` for Hopf/Tutte/quantum-graph compatibility.

Exit: novelty wording is claim-specific and source-backed.

## Phase 4 — next mathematics

### 4A. (c_8)

- derive candidate product formula or directly compute iterated normalized
  commutators;
- determine order;
- compute the top coefficient if proportional to (d^{\otimes4});
- record any lower-order correction basis required.

### 4B. general (c_{2k})

Only after (c_8):
- formulate a precise recurrence or obstruction;
- distinguish simple-cycle collisions from closed-walk convolution;
- state a conjecture only at the strongest level supported by the data/proof.

### 4C. other characters

Classify derivations at selected natural multiplicative characters only where
there is a graph-theoretic interpretation.

### 4D. algebraic bridge

Test actual bialgebra compatibility equations before using “Hopf extension”
language.

## Phase 5 — formalization

Formalize only stable interfaces:

1. graph ring/polynomial specialization with imported unique factorization;
2. derivations on the polynomial presentation;
3. spectral Kronecker-sum convolution;
4. one invariant-specific theorem after literature/status stabilization.

a separate domination-specific proof.
